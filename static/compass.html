Compass Deviation

Deviation = compass heading (true) minus course over ground, circularly folded to [-180°, 180°) and averaged per 10° heading sector. Only underway samples (not moored) at or above the speed threshold are included, so wake/current noise at low speed doesn't dominate the curve. A real compass deviation curve should be stable across trips in different locations — if a sector's bias shifts between date ranges, suspect current/set instead, or a nearby object that moved. See docs/ev1-compass-deviation-investigation.md for background.
